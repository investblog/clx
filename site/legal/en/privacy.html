Privacy

Version of 1 November 2026.

What clx is

clx is an open visit counter and short links. The clx worker and its database are installed in your own Cloudflare account: your sites' and links' traffic goes there, not through clx.cx. The code is open: github.com/investblog/clx.

What clx.cx keeps about you

  • your e-mail address, a hash of your password (PBKDF2), the time you signed in;
  • the working token of your Cloudflare account — encrypted only; a log of the calls it made on your behalf (90 days);
  • your sites, links and rules;
  • totals of your sites and links: views, bots and visitors by hour (2 days) and by day (400 days) — no pages, sources or other breakdowns;
  • your balance: its journal, your top-ups with the payment's id at Trybit or Telegram, and refund requests with the address you gave for them;
  • the partner program: your code, the partner who referred you, if any, your wallet and payout requests with their address.

clx.cx sets the sign-in session cookie (HttpOnly, this site only) and, after a visit by a partner link (?ref=), the cookie clx_ref with that partner's or promo code for 30 days, so that an account made then is credited to the partner or gets the promo. The Russian pages (ru.clx.cx) keep such a code for the tab, to pass it on to clx.cx. There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

Visitors of your sites

The counter sets no cookies and loads nothing from third-party addresses. Visit data — page, source, country, device type, browser and system — is kept in the database in your Cloudflare account, not on clx.cx. The IP address and User-Agent are used in memory only: to count a day's unique visitors they are hashed with a salt of the day; the salt and the hashes are deleted once the day is closed. Only totals go to clx.cx. A site not on Cloudflare (Pro) sends its visits to a host of your own Cloudflare zone, where the same worker counts them.

For your sites' visitors you are the controller of their data, and clx processes the totals. Mention the counter in your site's privacy policy.

E-mails

clx writes only when it has to: to confirm your address, to reset your password, about the state of your account. The e-mails go through Cloudflare Email Sending.

The Telegram bot

If you link Telegram, clx.cx keeps your Telegram user ID and username, and the label of the bot link you came by, if any (for example, the forum where you found clx). A question sent with /support goes to clx's support group in Telegram with your Telegram name and ID and, if you are signed in, your address, plan and that label; the group's members see it, and the messages stay in Telegram.

Deletion

Disconnecting a Cloudflare account removes the clx worker, database and routes from it; the totals on clx.cx are kept 30 days and then deleted. Deleting your clx account deletes it and all its totals at once; it is refused while money is on the balance or the partner wallet, or still moving (the terms list when). The record of its payments, refunds and partner money is kept for accounting, without your e-mail address or the addresses you gave. A Telegram Stars payment is kept by its Telegram payment id, with the payer's Telegram ID. A copy of the clx.cx database is made every night for recovery and kept 30 days: what is deleted leaves the copies as they expire.

Processors

clx.cx runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1, KV, R2 for the nightly copy, Email Sending, Turnstile). The bot and support run on Telegram. Crypto top-ups go through Trybit, which sees the invoice's sum and the paying wallet; Stars are paid through Telegram.
