Privacy

Version of 7 October 2026.

What clx is

clx is an open visit counter and short links. The clx worker and its database are installed in your own Cloudflare account: your sites' and links' traffic goes there, not through clx.cx. The code is open: github.com/investblog/clx.

What clx.cx keeps about you

  • your e-mail address, a hash of your password (PBKDF2), the time you signed in;
  • the working token of your Cloudflare account — encrypted only; a log of the calls it made on your behalf (90 days);
  • your sites, links and rules;
  • totals of your sites and links: views, bots and visitors by hour (2 days) and by day (400 days) — no pages, sources or other breakdowns.

clx.cx sets one cookie — the sign-in session (HttpOnly, this site only). There are no ads and no third-party analytics.

Visitors of your sites

The counter sets no cookies and loads nothing from other addresses. Visit data — page, source, country, device type, browser and system — is kept in the database in your Cloudflare account, not on clx.cx. The IP address and User-Agent are used in memory only: to count a day's unique visitors they are hashed with a salt of the day; the salt and the hashes are deleted once the day is closed. Only totals go to clx.cx.

For your sites' visitors you are the controller of their data, and clx processes the totals. Mention the counter in your site's privacy policy.

E-mails

clx writes only when it has to: to confirm your address, to reset your password, about the state of your account. The e-mails go through Cloudflare Email Sending.

Deletion

Disconnecting a Cloudflare account removes the clx worker, database and routes from it; the totals on clx.cx are kept 30 days and then deleted. Deleting your clx account deletes it and all its totals at once.

Processors

clx.cx runs on Cloudflare (Workers, D1, KV, Email Sending, Turnstile).
