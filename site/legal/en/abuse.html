Abuse

clx short links and counters run on their owners' domains and in their owners' Cloudflare accounts — clx.cx does not carry their traffic. So a report is handled fastest by the domain's owner or by Cloudflare (abuse.cloudflare.com).

If a link on your domain or someone else's leads to phishing, malware or other abuse and was made with clx, write to abuse@clx.cx: the link's address, where it leads, when you saw it. We disable accounts that use clx this way.
