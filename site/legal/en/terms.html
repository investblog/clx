The service

clx.cx is a free service: a visit counter and short links that run in your Cloudflare account. It is provided "as is", with no guarantee of availability and no liability for lost profit. The code is open under the AGPL-3.0 license — you can run your own copy.

Your Cloudflare account

By connecting an account you give clx a token with narrow rights: workers, D1, worker routes, and reading the account's zones, analytics and settings. clx puts there only its own worker clx-edge, its database and the routes of your sites and links, and changes nothing beyond that. Cloudflare quotas (requests, D1 writes) are spent in your account; clx warns you when it is time for a paid Cloudflare plan. You can revoke the token in Cloudflare at any time.

Links and sites

Short links live on your domains, and where they lead is your responsibility. clx must not be used for phishing, malware, spam or anything that breaks the law or Cloudflare's rules. Report abuse on the Abuse page; an abusing account may be disabled.

Changes
