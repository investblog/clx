The service

clx.cx is a visit counter and short links that run in your Cloudflare account, run by 301ST Ltd. The free plan costs nothing; Pro is paid from a balance (below). The service is provided "as is", with no guarantee of availability and no liability for lost profit. The code is open under the AGPL-3.0 license — you can run your own copy.

Your Cloudflare account

By connecting an account you give clx a token with narrow rights: workers, D1, worker routes, and reading the account's zones, analytics and settings. clx puts there only its own worker clx-edge, its database and the routes of your sites and links, and changes nothing beyond that. Cloudflare quotas (requests, D1 writes) are spent in your account; clx warns you when it is time for a paid Cloudflare plan. You can revoke the token in Cloudflare at any time.

Links and sites

Short links live on your domains, and where they lead is your responsibility. clx must not be used for phishing, malware, spam or anything that breaks the law or Cloudflare's rules. Report abuse on the Abuse page; an abusing account may be disabled.

Pro and the balance

  • Pro costs $9 per 30 days: $0.30 a day, taken from your clx balance in US dollars while Pro is on. Switching it on needs at least $9 on the balance and a confirmed e-mail address, and takes the day at once; then each next day (UTC) is taken in its turn. Switched off, Pro ends after the day already paid.
  • The balance is topped up with cryptocurrency through Trybit — USDT (TRON, BNB Smart Chain, Ethereum) or USDC (Ethereum), whole dollars from $9 to $1,000, an invoice valid for one hour — or with Telegram Stars in the bot @clxcxbot (750 Stars for $9, 2,500 Stars for $30). What arrives is credited; the fees of the network and of your wallet are yours. A payment that does not match its invoice is checked by hand and credited for what arrived, or returned.
  • The balance pays only for clx and earns no interest; it leaves only as a refund (below).
  • If the balance does not cover a day, Pro keeps working for 3 more days and clx tells you by e-mail and in Telegram. Without a top-up the account then returns to the free plan: what is beyond its limits is paused — not counted, not deleted — and works again once Pro is on.
